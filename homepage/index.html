PROJECT 01: FALL 2026

Interactive iPod

For this project, I created an interactive digital iPod inspired by music players that were popular in the early 2000s. I wanted to bring a piece of technology back to life through code, keeping the design simple and making the controls easy to explore. Visitors can move through the menu, select songs, use the playback buttons, and see album artwork on the screen. The project grew from my interest in music devices like CD players and radios, which led me to choose the iPod as my inspiration. I would like to expand it with more songs and playlist options eventually.

PROJECT 02: FALL 2026

Memory Box

For Project 2, I created a digital memory box that brings together objects, images, and small moments that hold personal meaning. The website is designed like opening a box of keepsakes, with each section focusing on a different memory, including movies, music, travel, photography, and things from my childhood. I wanted the experience to feel personal and nostalgic rather than like a traditional portfolio. I also used a p5.js background with falling Polaroid-style images to add movement to the site. Each page gives visitors a closer look at something I've chosen to keep and remember through a simple, interactive layout.
